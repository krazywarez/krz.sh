#+TITLE: hellsite. Privacy Policy
#+DESCRIPTION: Privacy policy for hellsite., the Tumblr client for iPhone, iPad and Mac.
#+WEIGHT: 6
#+APP: apps/hellsite/index.org

/last updated: 7 november 2026/

hellsite. has no krz accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app
purchases, and no third-party trackers or SDKs. there is no krz server: the app
talks to Tumblr, and to Apple for the features listed below. we collect
nothing.

* tumblr
you sign in with your Tumblr account in a browser sheet run by Apple; your
password goes to Tumblr, never to the app. Tumblr returns a sign-in token,
which the app keeps in the device's keychain and uses for every request. the
sign-in page returns to the app through a krz.sh address that Apple's sheet
catches on the device; that address is never loaded from our server.

everything the app shows and does goes straight to Tumblr: reading your
dashboard, blogs, tags and activity, and liking, reblogging, following,
posting and blocking. what you post and do is handled by Tumblr under
[[https://www.tumblr.com/privacy][Tumblr's privacy policy]]. requests carry the
app's name and version and no other identifiers. as with any internet request,
Tumblr's servers receive your IP address.

* posts and images on your device
posts and images from Tumblr are cached on the device to save requests and are
deleted after 72 hours at most, as Tumblr's terms for apps require. signing out of
every account deletes the image cache.

* kept on your device
your settings, saved tags, tag presets and post templates, mutes and content
warnings, and posts that couldn't be sent yet (the outbox) stay on the device.
the outbox, your blog names and today's posting limits are in a container
shared with the app's widget and share extension, so those can show them; the
widget and the share extension make no requests of their own.

* iCloud
the app syncs a few things between your own devices through your iCloud
account (Apple's key-value store): where you stopped reading in each feed (a
post id and its time, not the post), your personal lists (blog names), and the
read-later shelf (blog names and post ids, not the posts). these are stored by
Apple under your Apple Account and are not visible to us. signing out of iCloud
on a device stops the sync there.

* handoff
when you move from one of your devices to another, Handoff passes the screen
you were on and, for the dashboard, where you stopped reading. this goes
between your own devices through Apple.

* notifications
if you allow notifications, the app checks your Tumblr activity in the
background, at most as often as iOS allows, and shows notifications created on
the device. nothing goes to a push server.

* photos
the app reads only the photos you pick when posting. if you save an image from
a post, it asks for permission to add to your library and cannot see the rest
of it.

* changes
if this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
