Privacy policies for our iOS apps. The short version: there are no krz accounts,
no analytics, no ads, and no third-party trackers or SDKs. Your data stays on
your device, apart from the requests an app makes to the service you point it at
and a small number of named exceptions. Each policy below lists its own.

gitbay’s policy lives with the forge:
[[https://gitbay.org/privacy][gitbay.org/privacy]].

* This website
krz.sh is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or
trackers. It is served through Cloudflare, which handles your network (IP)
address to deliver the pages and protect the site, under the
[[https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/][Cloudflare privacy policy]].

* Purchases
*orgstar+ for Mac* is sold by Paddle, the merchant of record. Paddle collects
your payment and billing details under the
[[https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy][Paddle privacy notice]]; we never see
your card. We receive from Paddle what we need to support the purchase: your
email address, country, and the transaction. The license key page reads your
email address and transaction ID from Paddle and puts them in the key, which
stays on your Mac and is checked there, offline. We keep no database of keys;
Paddle keeps the purchase record.

*Purchases in the App Store* are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name,
email address or payment details, only Apple's sales reports.

If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you and don't share it.
Questions: [[mailto:root@krz.sh][root@krz.sh]].
