_last updated: 11 october 2026_

orgstar is an editor for Org mode files. it reads and writes the .org files in
folders you choose, on your device. we operate no servers for it, and we receive
none of your data.

## what we collect

nothing. no account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party trackers or SDKs.

## what stays on your device

your notes stay in the folders you add, in iCloud Drive or in another app's
storage, and are synced, if at all, by Apple or by the app that keeps that
folder. orgstar keeps an index of those files for search and the agenda, a
Spotlight index, the data its widgets read, and its settings, all on your
device. deleting the app removes them; your files stay where they are.

## calendar and notifications

if you turn on calendar events in the agenda, orgstar asks for access to your
calendars and only reads them, to show events alongside your entries. it never
changes or sends them anywhere. reminders before agenda entries are local
notifications scheduled on your device.

## network connections

orgstar makes no network requests of its own. links you tap open in your
browser or the app they belong to. the share extension and Shortcuts action
hand text and links to orgstar on your device.

one exception: exporting a file to PDF, RTF, or plain text lays it out with
Apple's WebKit, which loads what the exported page refers to. that can mean
images your file links to on the web, anything in the file's `#+HTML_HEAD`, and,
for files with math, the MathJax script from the jsDelivr CDN
(cdn.jsdelivr.net). those servers see your network (IP) address and the
requested address under their own policies. HTML and Markdown export make no
requests.
