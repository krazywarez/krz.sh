#+TITLE: gitbay Privacy Policy
#+DESCRIPTION: Privacy policy for gitbay, the iOS client for gitbay forges.
#+WEIGHT: 7
#+APP: apps/gitbay/index.org

/last updated: 28 november 2026/

gitbay is a native iOS client for a gitbay instance you name at sign-in. it has
no account of its own, no sign-up, and no server of ours behind it: it talks
only to that instance, over HTTPS.

* what we collect
nothing. there is no krz account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, no
ads, and no third-party trackers or SDKs.

* what stays on your device
signing in stores one API token in the iOS Keychain. which account is active is
kept in app preferences; nothing else is kept on the device. removing the
account deletes the token from the Keychain. tokens are minted over SSH or on
the instance's settings page, and =gitbay auth token revoke= ends the app's
access immediately.

* network connections
every request goes to the instance you signed in to, carries your token, and is
governed by that instance's own terms and privacy policy. we are not a party to
that traffic and never see it.

* notifications
if you turn on notifications, the app registers your device with that
instance, and the instance sends each notification through Apple's push
service. the notification text names the repository and the item, private
repositories included, and appears on the lock screen. removing your devices in
the instance's settings, or turning notifications off, stops it.

* changes
if this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
