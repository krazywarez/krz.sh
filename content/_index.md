## about

krz builds native apps and self-hosted, privacy-respecting frontends. two people, a few servers, no investors. local-first data, few dependencies, no ads, no tracking.

## principles

- fixed scope. a tool that's done stays done.
- local-first. your data stays on your device, not our servers.
- fewer dependencies. less to break, less to trust.
- maintenance over features. we'd rather fix than pad.
