Mechanism runs the planar linkages, gear trains and ratchets from Henry T.
Brown's *Five Hundred and Seven Mechanical Movements* (1868). Each one is a
working mechanism drawn as draughting, numbered as the book numbers it, on a
catalogue you pinch across like a sheet of plates. Every movement can also be
driven without a drag.

No accounts, ads or tracking, and no network access.
