hellsite. is a Tumblr client written in Swift and SwiftUI for iPhone, iPad and
Mac. It covers what Tumblr's public API offers: the dashboard, blogs, tags and
posts in every NPF layout; likes, reblogs and follows; a block editor that
writes NPF directly, with drafts, the queue, scheduling, submissions and asks;
activity with notifications; communities; blocks and filtered tags. It does not
scrape, use private endpoints or show web pages in place of missing features.

The dashboard shows what Tumblr sends, in order. A read marker remembers where
you stopped, so after a day away the app offers to jump there and read up to
the newest post, and it syncs between your devices through iCloud. Calm mode
hides note counts, keeps GIFs and video still until you tap them, and holds
new posts behind a button.
Content warnings and your own mutes cover a post behind a tap instead of
hiding it.

There is a gallery of photo and video posts, a reading view for long text,
personal lists that read up to 20 blogs as one feed, a read-later shelf, and
tools for your own blog: post performance, a queue planner and bulk retagging.
On iPad and Mac the app has a sidebar, a wide feed and the post beside it, with
windows for each account on Mac.

No krz account, ads or tracking. You sign in with Tumblr, and the app talks to
Tumblr directly.
