Fold animates traditional origami models step by step. Each model is one
dataset: the app draws the Yoshizawa–Randlett diagram for a step and folds the
paper from the same geometry, so the diagram and the model cannot disagree.
Orbit and zoom at any step to look under a flap.

No accounts, ads or tracking, and no network access.
